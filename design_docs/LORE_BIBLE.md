# GOBLIN CHAIN — Lore Bible

> *"In the beginning, there was a whitepaper. Nobody read it."*

---

## The World

**Year:** 2049. The fiat economy collapsed three years ago when an AI hedge fund accidentally shorted every currency on Earth simultaneously. In the power vacuum, crypto became the only surviving financial system — but not the kind anyone wanted.

The world is now run by **The Blockchain Council**, a shadowy cabal of anonymous wallet addresses that no one can trace and no one elected. Governments are gone. Banks are museums. The only law is the ledger.

Beneath the gleaming towers of legitimate crypto institutions lies **The Underhive** — a neon-lit, cable-tangled maze of repurposed server rooms and makeshift mining farms. This is where **Goblins** operate.

**Goblins** aren't a species. It's a slang term for the bottom-feeders of the crypto world — small-time miners, hustlers, rug-pullers, and scammers who are too stubborn, too broke, or too unhinged to go straight. They call themselves goblins with pride. In a world where everything is fake, at least goblins are honest about being dishonest.

---

## The Underhive

The game takes place in a section of The Underhive called **Sector 7G** — a condemned server farm the size of a city block. It was supposed to be demolished, but someone bribed the demolition bot with 0.003 BTC, so it just stands there, vibrating.

Sector 7G features:

- **The Pit** — The central atrium where goblins gather, trade, and occasionally fistfight over decimal places.
- **Rig Row** — Narrow corridors lined with stolen GPU racks and overclocked mining rigs held together with zip ties and prayers.
- **The Exchange** — A neon-soaked trading floor that's really just a loading dock with screens bolted to the walls.
- **The Furnace** — The bottom level. Cooling failed years ago. Rigs here mine faster but are more vulnerable to overheating.
- **Rooftop** — Open air. Good for EMPs. Bad for privacy. The satellite dishes up here were once used for legitimate internet; now they broadcast pump-and-dump schemes.

## The Coins

### GBC — GoblinCoin
**Ticker:** GBC
**Founded:** 2047
**Whitepaper:** "We'll figure it out"
**Tagline:** *"The coin for the rest of us."*

GoblinCoin is the native currency of the Underhive. It was created by an anonymous developer known only as **SatoshiGoblin**, who posted the genesis block, said "good luck lol," and was never heard from again. GBC has no real utility, no governance model, and no roadmap. This is precisely why goblins trust it.

GBC is backed by nothing except collective delusion and hash power. Its price fluctuates wildly based on vibes, memes, and whether someone accidentally typed an extra zero in a trade.

**Fun facts:**
- The GBC logo is a green diamond because the original designer only had MS Paint
- GBC's all-time high was caused by a typo in a whale's sell order
- The "G" in GBC officially stands for nothing

---

### GOBETH — Goblin Ethereum
**Ticker:** GOBETH
**Founded:** 2048
**Whitepaper:** 47 pages, all footnotes
**Tagline:** *"Smart contracts, dumb decisions."*

GOBETH is a fork of a fork of a fork of Ethereum. Nobody remembers what was changed at each stage, so the codebase is essentially a palimpsest of conflicting smart contracts. Despite this, GOBETH has the second-highest market cap in the Underhive because its name sounds legitimate.

GOBETH's "smart contracts" are famous for having bugs that accidentally became features. One contract meant to handle escrow instead created an infinite loop that mines 0.0001 GOBETH every time someone sneezes within 50 feet of a node.

---

### RUGDAO — Rug Pull DAO
**Ticker:** RUGDAO
**Founded:** 2048 (refounded 14 times)
**Whitepaper:** "Trust us bro"
**Tagline:** *"Transparency through obscurity."*

RUGDAO is a "decentralized autonomous organization" where every member is anonymous and every vote is rigged. It has been rug-pulled 14 times by 14 different founders, and each time the community simply re-launches with slightly more paranoia and slightly less treasury.

RUGDAO's governance model is unique: proposals are voted on, but the results are interpreted by a Magic 8-Ball that the current DAO leader keeps on their desk. The community considers this an improvement over the previous system, which was a coin flip.

RUGDAO trades at fractions of a penny but has survived longer than 99% of legitimate crypto projects, which the community considers a point of pride.

---

### PONZI — PonziToken
**Ticker:** PONZI
**Founded:** 2046
**Whitepaper:** A pyramid diagram with "TRUST ME" written on it
**Tagline:** *"Early investors win. That's you, right?"*

PonziToken is the most expensive coin in the Underhive by face value, trading at roughly 12.5x the price of GBC. Nobody can explain why. The tokenomics are a literal Ponzi scheme — the whitepaper admits this on page one — but the community argues this makes it the most honest project in crypto.

PONZI has a "reverse burn" mechanism: instead of destroying tokens to increase scarcity, it mints new tokens every time someone complains about the price. The community calls this "infinite supply energy."

The PONZI community is called **The Foundation** and they greet each other with "to the top of the pyramid."

---

### COPE — CopeCoin
**Ticker:** COPE
**Founded:** 2049
**Whitepaper:** A single page that just says "it's fine"
**Tagline:** *"Everything is fine."*

COPE is the cheapest coin in the Underhive, trading at 0.001x GBC. It was created by a goblin who lost everything in a RUGDAO rug pull and decided to "monetize the sadness." COPE has no utility except as a measure of community despair.

COPE's price is inversely correlated with market confidence — when everything crashes, COPE moons, because that's when people need to cope the most. This accidentally makes it the best hedge in the Underhive, which makes COPE holders feel conflicted.

The COPE community calls themselves **Copers** and their rallying cry is "We're all gonna make it (we're not)."

## Character Archetypes

Goblins come in many forms. These are the archetypes recognized in Sector 7G:

### The Miner
*"More rigs. Always more rigs."*

The backbone of the Underhive. Miners believe that hash rate is the only honest metric and that if you can't touch the hardware, you don't own the coin. They hoard GPUs the way dragons hoard gold and will fight anyone who threatens their uptime.

### The Trader
*"Buy low. Sell high. Or the other way around, whatever."*

Traders are pure capitalists. They don't care what a coin does, only what it's worth in five minutes. They live in the Exchange, surrounded by screens, running mental math that's usually wrong but occasionally spectacularly right.

### The Saboteur
*"If I can't win, nobody can."*

Saboteurs are chaos agents. They hoard EMPs, wait for Chaos phase, and make it their personal mission to reduce the Underhive's total net worth to zero. They consider "most rigs destroyed" a more meaningful achievement than "most GBC earned."

### The Whale
*"I simply have more money than you."*

Whales are goblins who hit it big on an early trade and now manipulate the market through sheer volume. They can tank a coin's price by selling, or moon it by buying, and they do both for entertainment.

### The Degen
*"I don't understand what I'm buying and that's part of the appeal."*

Degens buy everything, trade constantly, and react to every market headline as if it's the most important event in financial history. They have the most fun and the worst portfolio.

## Random Event Lore

### Hash Boost — "Solar Flare"
*Deep space radiation from a nearby solar flare temporarily supercharges every mining rig in the Underhive. Scientists say this is impossible. Goblins say scientists are poor.*

### Power Outage — "Rolling Blackout"
*The Underhive's power grid, which is 40% stolen electricity and 60% wishful thinking, hiccups again. Random rigs go dark. The goblins whose rigs stay on pretend they planned for this.*

### Gold Rush — "Rare Block"
*A once-in-a-thousand block appears on the chain — denser, richer, worth more than a week of normal mining. The goblin with the most rigs finds it first, because that's how probability works and also how unfairness works.*

### Tax Audit — "The IRS Remembers"
*Nobody knows how the IRS still exists. Nobody knows how they found the Underhive. But a drone just delivered a tax notice to the richest goblin in Sector 7G, and 10% of their wallet is now "evidence."*

### Whale Alert — "Anonymous Buyer"
*A massive buy order appears from wallet address 0xDEAD...BEEF. No one knows who owns it. The price spikes. Conspiracy theories multiply faster than the coins.*

### Liquidity Crisis — "Exchange Fee Spike"
*The exchange's automated fee algorithm, written by an intern who no longer works here, has decided that all transactions now cost 15 GBC. Everyone pays. The intern's code does not accept feedback.*

### Airdrop — "Mystery Drop"
*An unidentified smart contract just distributed free coins to every wallet in the Underhive. Nobody knows where they came from. Half the goblins are thrilled. The other half are terrified. Both reactions are correct.*

### Server Fire — "Thermal Runaway"
*The Furnace level finally lives up to its name. A cooling pipe bursts, temperatures spike, and every rig in the Underhive emergency-shuts to avoid literal meltdown. The goblins who built rigs on the Rooftop feel briefly smug.*

### SEC Raid — "Regulators Inbound"
*The Blockchain Council's enforcement division — yes, the decentralized system has an enforcement division, nobody talks about this — has identified the richest goblin as a "person of interest." 25% of their holdings are seized and redistributed, because apparently that's what decentralization means now.*

### Meme Viral Moment — "Going Viral"
*One goblin's hastily photoshopped meme of a goblin riding a rocket hits the Underhive's social feed and goes nuclear. Donations pour in from impressed degens across the network. Being funny is, apparently, a viable financial strategy.*

## Market Headlines — Extended Lore

### Crash Headlines
Each crash has a story behind it:

- **"GoblinCoin founder seen buying ramen in bulk"** — SatoshiGoblin was spotted at a bulk food store. Insider selling confirmed (it wasn't, he was just hungry).
- **"Whale dumps entire wallet, cites 'bad vibes'"** — Wallet 0xBAAD posted a single transaction memo: "nah." The market lost 40%.
- **"Smart contract found to contain only 'lol'"** — A code audit of GOBETH's core contract revealed the execution logic was a single comment: `// lol`. The auditor quit.
- **"Market manipulation? No, just regular manipulation"** — The Blockchain Council investigated insider trading and concluded that since everyone is insider trading, no one is.
- **"Anonymous dev tweets 'oops' then deletes account"** — A developer with commit access to GBC's repo tweeted "oops" at 3 AM, deleted their account, and has not been seen since. Their last commit was "fix: nothing."
- **"Audit reveals blockchain is just a Google Sheet"** — A whistleblower leaked that RUGDAO's "distributed ledger" is a shared Google Sheet with comment permissions enabled.
- **"Liquidity pool drained by 'definitely_not_admin'"** — The RUGDAO liquidity pool was emptied by a wallet named `definitely_not_admin`. The community voted to "look into it" and then didn't.

### Moon Headlines
- **"Influencer accidentally promotes GoblinCoin instead of rival"** — A crypto influencer with 2M followers copy-pasted the wrong ticker into their "100x GEM ALERT" video. GBC pumped 300% before the correction tweet.
- **"Elon tweets goblin emoji"** — No further context needed. The market did the rest.
- **"GoblinCoin accidentally listed on real exchange"** — A major exchange's auto-listing bot somehow indexed GBC. It was delisted within hours, but the price never came back down.
- **"AI bot stuck in buy loop, refuses to stop"** — A trading bot's exit condition had a bug. It bought GBC every 0.3 seconds for 47 minutes. The developer tried to kill the process but the bot had sudo.
- **"'Diamond hands' meme goes viral, price follows"** — A low-effort meme featuring a goblin holding diamonds became the most-shared image on the Underhive network. FOMO did the rest.
- **"Fortune cookie predicted GoblinCoin would moon today"** — A goblin posted a photo of a fortune cookie reading "Your investments will pay off." The community took this as financial advice.
